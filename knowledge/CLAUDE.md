### SDV Offerings
Embitel's SDV offerings span the full vehicle software/hardware stack, organized around four areas:
1. **Vehicle software platforms & middleware** — AUTOSAR-based development (MCAL drivers, RTE integration, complex device drivers, flash loader tools) [BROWSED: case studies], board support packages (BSP), Android Automotive and Linux porting, telematics SDK/platform, diagnostics & communication software stacks (UDS, DoIP, J1939, CAN/CAN FD, ISOBUS, OBD2, J2534) [BROWSED: case studies], flash bootloader/ECU reprogramming.
2. **In-vehicle experience** — infotainment (dual-display systems, head-up displays with gesture control) [BROWSED: case studies], digital instrument clusters, HMI/UI development, in-vehicle personalization, ADAS & driver monitoring system (DMS) validation.
3. **Electrification (EV)** — EV development, motor control for EV drivetrains (BLDC controllers, hub motors) [BROWSED: case studies], traction inverters & DC-DC converters, battery management systems, on-board charger software, EV charging station network solutions, electric vehicle control units.
4. **Safety, security & compliance** — ISO 26262 (ASIL D) software and hardware development, functional safety analysis (FMEA/FMEDA, mutation testing) [BROWSED: case studies] and testing, automotive cybersecurity (aligned to ISO/SAE 21434 and UN R155) [BROWSED: cybersecurity page], trusted applications, PKI services, penetration testing, SIL/MIL/HIL/XiL testing, ECU testing & verification, body control module (BCM) design.

Underpinning these: OTA/FOTA update solutions (own accelerator product, AWS-based, Uptane-ready — see below) [BROWSED], DevOps services, and IoT/cloud application development extended into the vehicle context. **[SYNOPSIS-RECOVERED — see "Sources Not Fully Accessible" below]** Embitel's own SDV services page meta-description also names **digital twin integration** as an explicit part of its end-to-end SDV messaging, alongside OTA and ADAS — not corroborated on any individual service page that loaded in full, so flagged here as a positioning claim to verify/watch rather than a fully documented capability.

**[BROWSED — ADAS page] ADAS is called out as a strategic focus in its own right:** system/software architecture for radar, camera, LiDAR and ultrasonic sensors; embedded software for ECUs and perception modules; E/E integration across CAN, LIN, FlexRay and Automotive Ethernet; ADAS validation (KPI evaluation, SIL/HIL/XiL simulation, video-based validation); radar/camera/ultrasonic recompute systems; multi-sensor fusion for urban and highway scenarios; and emerging coverage of V2X communication and cloud-based ADAS.

**[BROWSED — telematics platform page] Two named proprietary accelerator products** appear to be flagship IP: a **Telematics SDK** (claimed 50% reduction in time-to-market via pre-built middleware for power management, location services, driver monitoring, data logging) and an **OTA/FOTA Accelerator** (AWS-based, microservices architecture using Kubernetes/Kafka/Lambda, A/B partition updates, automatic rollback, Uptane-ready security, tested across 350+ scenarios and 10,000+ devices, built to manage millions of connected vehicles).
